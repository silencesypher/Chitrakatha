how does this work?
One player draws a word — a character, weapon, place or episode from Purāṇic lore — while everyone else guesses. The host picks English (IAST, with ā, ś, ṣ, ṛ and friends) or देवनागरी (Devanagari) for the round when setting up the room, and an on-screen keyboard for that script sits below the guess box, so no special keyboard is needed either way. Fastest correct guesses score the most. Share the room code with your circle and everyone joins from their own phone or laptop.
